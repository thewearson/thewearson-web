Account Freezing, Deactivation, and Deletion

THE WEARS ON distinguishes between temporarily freezing or deactivating an account and permanently deleting an account.

8.1 Account Freezing and Deactivation

Users may freeze or deactivate their accounts through the account-management functionality available in the application.

Freezing or deactivating an account does not automatically cancel an active Premium subscription.

If a user has an active subscription, billing may continue according to the subscription terms until the subscription is canceled through Apple’s subscription management system.

Users who wish to stop future subscription renewals must separately cancel their subscription through their Apple Account subscription settings.

8.2 Permanent Account Deletion

Users may request permanent deletion of their THE WEARS ON accounts through the account-deletion functionality provided in the application.

Following a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information in accordance with applicable law and our data-retention obligations.

Certain information may remain temporarily in backups or may be retained where required by law or necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.

8.3 Subscription Status After Account Deletion

Deleting a THE WEARS ON account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store subscription.

Users must separately cancel their subscription through Apple’s subscription management settings to prevent future renewals.

Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate processes.

Deleting an account does not automatically terminate an existing subscription or guarantee a refund for previous payments.

Users should review their Apple Account subscription settings to confirm that their subscription has been canceled.
